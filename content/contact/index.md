        ## Prospective Students

        BCAS Lab welcomes motivated students interested in analog/mixed-signal IC design, biomedical circuits and systems, wireless power and data transfer, neural interfaces, neuromodulation ICs, and wearable/implantable bioelectronic systems.

        Students interested in joining BCAS Lab are encouraged to contact Prof. Byunghun Lee by email with a brief introduction, CV, transcript, and research interests.
