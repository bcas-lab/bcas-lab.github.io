Jiyoung Moon and co-authors' paper, **"Rapid Moisture Removal and Low-Temperature Shoe Drying Using a Stackable and Reconfigurable RF Heating Appliance,"** was accepted for publication in *IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics*.

This work was conducted in collaboration between **BCAS Lab at Hanyang University** and **Samsung Research (SR), Samsung Electronics**.

The work presents a 13.56-MHz RF shoe-drying system featuring stackable and reconfigurable electrode modules and a multi-unit impedance-matching network. The system accommodates different shoe shapes and quantities without additional retuning, achieving substantial moisture reduction while maintaining shoe-surface temperatures below 25°C under the tested conditions.

**Journal:** IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics (IF: 10.9, JCR Top 3.1%)

**Accepted:** November 10, 2026
