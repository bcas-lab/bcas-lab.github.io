Junhyuck Lee and co-authors' paper, **"A 13.56-MHz Wireless Power and Synchronized Full-duplex Data Transceiver IC Using a Single Inductive Link for Wearable Biomedical Applications,"** was accepted for publication in *IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits*.

The work presents a 13.56-MHz integrated transceiver that supports wireless power transfer and synchronized full-duplex data communication through a single inductive link for wearable biomedical applications.

**Authors:** Junhyuck Lee, Yemin Kim, Dongrim Kim, Jaejun Kil, Woobin Hyung, and Byunghun Lee  
**Journal:** IEEE Journal of Solid-State Circuits  
**Accepted:** July 28, 2026
