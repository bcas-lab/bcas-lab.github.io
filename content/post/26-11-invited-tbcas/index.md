Two papers presented by the **BCAS Lab** at **IEEE BioCAS 2026** have been invited to submit extended versions to the **IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Circuits and Systems (TBioCAS) Special Issue**.

<!--more-->

**Invited Papers:**

1. **An Artifact-Tolerant Gain-Compression AFE with a Wide Dynamic Range for ECoG Recording**

2. **A Reconfigurable Single-Stage Wireless Receiver with Time-Multiplexed Load Regulation and Pulse Charging for Biomedical Applications**

The invitations were extended following positive evaluations of the conference papers. Extended manuscripts are expected to include significant technical enhancements and additional experimental results.

Congratulations to the BCAS members involved!
