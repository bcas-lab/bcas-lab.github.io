Yemin Kim and co-authors' paper, **"Minimally Invasive Square-based Temporal Interference Stimulation IC for Neuromodulation with Enhanced E-field Delivery,"** was accepted for publication in *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Circuits and Systems*.

The work presents a minimally invasive square-based TIS IC that integrates a 13.56-MHz inductive link for wireless power and data transfer with programmable two-channel stimulation. The proposed system employs fully digital ON/OFF-controlled H-bridge drivers to simplify stimulation waveform generation while enhancing E-field delivery for deep neuromodulation.

**Authors:** Yemin Kim, Junhyuck Lee, Jaejun Kil, Dongrim Kim, and Byunghun Lee

**Journal:** IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Circuits and Systems

**Accepted:** August 25, 2026
