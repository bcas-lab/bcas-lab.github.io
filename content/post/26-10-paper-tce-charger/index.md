Minsu Song and co-authors' paper, **"A Compact 92.4%-peak Efficiency Photovoltaic-powered Li-ion Battery Charger with Interleaved Pulse Charging and Built-in Resistance Detection,"** was accepted for publication in *IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics*.

The work presents a compact photovoltaic-powered Li-ion battery charger featuring interleaved pulse charging and built-in resistance detection, achieving a peak efficiency of 92.4%.

**Journal:** IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics (IF: 10.9, JCR Top 3.1%)

**Accepted:** October 25, 2026
