Five papers from the **BCAS Lab** have been accepted to the **2026 IEEE Biomedical Circuits and Systems Conference (BioCAS 2026)**, which will be held in Incheon, Korea, from October 8-10, 2026.

Among the five accepted papers, **four were selected for Lecture presentations** and **one for a Poster presentation**.

**Accepted papers:**

**1. Lecture Presentation**  
"An Artifact-Tolerant Gain-Compression AFE with a Wide Dynamic Range for ECoG Recording"

**2. Lecture Presentation**  
"A Single-Stage Wireless Multi-Channel Dynamic Supply Stimulator with 10-V Compliance for Temporal Interference Stimulation"

**3. Lecture Presentation**  
"A Peak-Controlled Bidirectional DC-DC Interface IC for Maximum Charge Recycling in Electrochemical CNT-Yarn Artificial Muscles"

**4. Lecture Presentation**  
"A Reconfigurable Single-Stage Wireless Receiver with Time-Multiplexed Load Regulation and Pulse Charging for Biomedical Applications"

**5. Poster Presentation**  
"A Miniaturized Wireless TIS System Using Dual-band Inductive Coupling for Power and Data Transfer"

These works cover several key research areas of the BCAS Lab, including **neural recording, temporal interference stimulation, wireless power and data transfer, and bioelectronic interfaces**.

Congratulations to all BCAS members and collaborators involved in these works!
