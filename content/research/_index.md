        BCAS Lab develops low-power integrated circuits and systems for next-generation biomedical applications. Our research spans from circuit-level design to chip implementation, PCB/system integration, measurement, and experimental validation.

        ## Research Areas

        ### Neural Interface ICs
        Low-noise and high-density integrated circuits for neural recording and stimulation.

        ### Wireless Power & Data Transfer
        Wireless power transfer and synchronized data communication systems for wearable and implantable biomedical devices.

        ### Neuromodulation Systems
        Miniaturized stimulation circuits and systems for precise and efficient neuromodulation.

        ### Sensor Interface and PVT Monitoring ICs
        Low-power analog/mixed-signal circuits for biomedical sensing, sensor interfaces, and on-chip monitoring.

        ### Wearable and Implantable Bioelectronics
        System-level integration of ICs, PCBs, coils, sensors, and bioelectronic platforms for real-world biomedical applications.
