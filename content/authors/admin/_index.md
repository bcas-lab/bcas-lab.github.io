Byunghun Lee is an Associate Professor at Hanyang University and the director of the Biomedical Circuits and Systems Lab. His research focuses on low-power integrated circuits and systems for neural interfaces, wireless power and data transfer, neuromodulation, and wearable/implantable bioelectronics.

The Biomedical Circuits and Systems Lab develops biomedical ICs and system-level platforms spanning circuit design, chip implementation, PCB/system integration, measurement, and experimental validation.
